Famous Indian Snacks

Kachori

Kachori is a popular Indian snack known for its crispy outer layer and spicy filling. It is usually stuffed with lentils, onions, or peas and deep-fried until golden brown. Kachoris are commonly served with chutneys or spicy potato curry and are enjoyed across India.

Samosa

Samosa is a triangular-shaped fried snack filled with spiced potatoes, peas, or meat. It has a crunchy outer crust and a flavorful filling inside. Samosas are one of the most loved street foods in India and are often served with mint or tamarind chutney.
